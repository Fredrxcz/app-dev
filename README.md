
# My Favorite Movie: Titanic

## About the Movie
Titanic is a romantic drama film released in 1997, directed by James Cameron. The movie tells the story of Jack Dawson and Rose DeWitt Bukater, two people from different social classes who fall in love aboard the RMS Titanic during its tragic voyage.

## Why I Like It
I like Titanic because it has a beautiful love story, emotional scenes, and meaningful lessons about love, sacrifice, and courage. The movie also shows how love can bring two people together despite their differences.

## Main Characters
- Jack Dawson
- Rose DeWitt Bukater
- Caledon Hockley
- Ruth DeWitt Bukater

## My Favorite Part
My favorite part is when Jack and Rose spend time together and share their dreams while aboard the Titanic. Their love story is touching and unforgettable.

## My Favorite Quote
> I'm the king of the world!

## My Rating
**10/10** - I highly recommend this movie because it is romantic, emotional, and unforgettable.

## Conclusion
Titanic is one of my favorite movies because it teaches us about true love, bravery, and the importance of appreciating every moment with the people we love.
